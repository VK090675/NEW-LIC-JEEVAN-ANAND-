Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LIC New Jeevan Anand Plan 715

What is LIC New Jeevan Anand Plan 715?

LIC New Jeevan Anand Plan 715 is an endowment assurance plan that provides financial protection against death throughout the policy term with the provision of lump sum payment at the end of selected policy term in case of survival. The unique feature is that life cover continues even after maturity till age 100.

What makes Jeevan Anand 715 different from other plans?

The key differentiator is the post-maturity life cover. After you receive the full maturity amount with bonuses, your life insurance cover for the Basic Sum Assured continues till age 100 without paying any further premium. This dual benefit is unique to Jeevan Anand.

What is the minimum and maximum sum assured?

The minimum Basic Sum Assured is Rs. 1,00,000. There is no maximum limit for Sum Assured, but it is subject to underwriting guidelines and income proof as per LIC norms.

Can I get a loan against this policy?

Yes, loan facility is available after the policy has acquired surrender value, i.e., after payment of 2 full years' premiums. The loan amount can be up to 90% of the Surrender Value for inforce policies.

What are the tax benefits available?

Premiums paid are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80C of Income Tax Act up to Rs. 1.5 Lakh per annum. The maturity proceeds and death benefits are completely tax-free under Section 10(10D), subject to conditions. Please consult your tax advisor for specific advice.

What happens if I stop paying premiums?

If premiums are not paid within the grace period, the policy lapses. However, if at least 2 full years' premiums have been paid, the policy acquires a paid-up value and will continue with reduced benefits. You can also revive a lapsed policy within 2 years by paying arrears with interest.
